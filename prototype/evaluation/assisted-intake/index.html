What failed in practice

Haiku: some source IDs did not match the IDs supplied to the model; one field had a value but an “ambiguous” status. Three strict value mismatches were rounding of 80 minutes to 1.3333 hours. Mistral: examples included 1,234 users becoming 1,234,000, 150 minutes becoming 150 hours, and a number without a unit being accepted as hours. These are substantive errors even when JSON and range checks pass. Gemini API failures remain in the denominators and have not been retried.

Production conditions

  • Enforce classification and approved-provider policy locally BEFORE any model egress; a model detecting confidentiality after upload is too late to protect that transmission.
  • Use enterprise SSO, tenant/partner access isolation, least privilege, managed credentials and encrypted storage/transport.
  • Minimize/redact content; no source content in operational logs; defined deletion for originals, outputs, backups and caches.
  • Restricted data that cannot leave NIG uses a separately approved private deployment or is blocked; never silently switch to another provider.
  • Review of downloaded artifacts remains BD responsibility. Download does not attest review or authorize distribution. Future external/API actions require destination and disclosure policies.

Recommendation and next decision

  • Quality versus availability: Gemini returned 221/221 correct field decisions and passed 117/120 full cases. Three 75-second timeouts count as failed attempts; p95 latency was 22.38 seconds. Test bounded same-provider retries, background jobs and useful failure messages before deployment. Retry cost and success were not measured.
  • Haiku passed 112/120 cases at $0.38 per 1,000 test-sized intakes, versus Gemini at $1.45. Its three strict numeric mismatches were rounding (80 minutes to 1.3333 hours), not substantive inventions. Unknown reference IDs and one inconsistent field status also failed checks. Move arithmetic to code, constrain reference IDs and define business precision, then retest on new held-out documents before considering a cheaper default.
  • Mistral was fastest and cheapest ($0.24 per 1,000) but produced 18 substantive incorrect values; 17 affected outputs still passed the mechanical validator. Do not trade trustworthy figures for small token savings. A different prompt/model or private deployment would require a new comparison.
  • Business scale: 100,000 intakes at this exact synthetic mix imply about $145 for Gemini tokens at current rates, versus $38 for Haiku. Published Gemini rates double from January 2027, implying about $290 for the same mix. These are planning extrapolations, not full-report or production bills; bigger documents, scans, retries, OCR, hosting, security and maintenance add cost.
  • Use deterministic parsing first and assisted intake only when normalization is needed. Request raw values, units and source IDs, perform conversions in code and preserve provenance. Validate or omit uncertain fields automatically; do not silently invent values and do not add a BD mapping-confirmation screen.
  • Before confidential use: verify no-training terms, actual retention/ZDR settings, permitted processing regions, partner restrictions and source permissions on the exact account and endpoint. Classify before egress, minimize content, protect local storage and disallow unapproved cross-provider fallback. A new enterprise endpoint needs another compatibility/performance check.
  • Keep the current reporting prototype unchanged. This separate benchmark does not establish complete narrative preservation, enterprise OCR quality, actual deletion or protection against all leaks. Independent human review of gold answers and a broader representative validation set remain pending.

Method and limits

30 development cases plus 10 frozen holdout cases, each repeated three times per model. All documents and gold answers were authored and checked by the assistant; independent human review is pending. Holdout cases were not used to tune the prompt but are not independently sourced. Repeated calls to the same case are not independent production samples.

Real XLSX, CSV, DOCX, TXT, VTT, SRT, a text PDF and five raster PDF fixtures were generated. Office/text files were parsed locally to cells/paragraphs/lines. Identical page PNGs from the synthetic raster PDFs were sent inline to all providers; no provider Files API or third-party OCR was used. These short artificial scans do not establish performance on difficult enterprise scans or handwriting. Legacy DOC is not tested.

Scope: numeric normalization, dates, commitment/status distinctions, references and explicit confidentiality/injection markers. Full narrative completeness, confidential cross-partner redaction, operational security, endpoint residency, deletion and human review time were not tested. Models used different low-cost reasoning settings; details and all outputs are retained. One serial worker per provider ran concurrently during development; holdout timing differed by provider. This is a small local comparison, not a controlled production load or capacity test. No retries or model fallback. Timeouts: Anthropic 90 seconds; the Gemini/Mistral HTTP helper uses 75 seconds. Failed-call charges without returned usage are unknown. A provenance scorer correction accepted a correct data row plus valid contextual header, uniformly across candidates.
