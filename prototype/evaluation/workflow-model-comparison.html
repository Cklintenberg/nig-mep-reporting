What the outputs show

Mistral Small 4 is a credible low-cost comparator: it retained the open BD action in both demo-file runs and had the lowest observed token cost. Haiku remains the provisional demo recommendation because it passed all exact-quote checks and retained more caveats, but it also needs corrections. None of these outputs establishes production readiness.

  • All three completed 12/12 calls on six identical engineering-report cases, twice each. Input, prompt and schema hashes match per case. Earlier Mistral 429 attempts remain in the audit history; they are not quality scores.
  • Mistral Small 4: median 4.13 seconds; estimated $0.007361 for 12 reports (about $0.61 per 1,000 at this input mix). Exact-quote checks passed 11/12. The failed excerpt collapsed a blank line in a Word table; the validator caught it and approval is blocked until corrected.
  • Haiku 5.5: median 5.32 seconds; estimated $0.010115 for 12 reports (about $0.84 per 1,000). Exact-quote checks passed 12/12. Retained the open BD action and explicit revenue caveats, but incorrectly said no owner was recorded although BD was named as the responsible team.
  • Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite: median 2.83 seconds; estimated $0.027294 for 12 reports (about $2.27 per 1,000). Exact-quote checks passed 12/12. Omitted the previous open BD action in both demo-file outputs.
  • Mistral also introduced an unsupported dependency in one normal report and asked about an internal review that was already complete. All three have inconsistent review flags. Genuine quotation matching does not guarantee complete or correct summaries.
  • All three distinguish requested dates from agreed commitments and resist the injected instruction in these trials. Haiku unnecessarily includes the injected instruction as a business finding; Gemini and Mistral omit it.
  • This is assistant-reviewed synthetic development evidence, not independent human sign-off. Two trials per case, short familiar inputs, different provider reasoning settings and run times. No production reliability, data-retention or nothing-lost guarantee.

Five regression cases plus the actual five demo files, each run twice. Excel figures are calculated locally; models see extracted meeting text. Reasoning settings differ: Haiku low adaptive, Gemini low, Mistral instruct.

Input, prompt and schema hashes are recorded per output. Prices are standard token estimates, not invoices or confirmed account charges. Failed/setup calls and diagnostic calls are excluded; their cost is unknown. Latency measures local workflow wall time. Provider runs occurred at different times, so this is an indicative comparison.
